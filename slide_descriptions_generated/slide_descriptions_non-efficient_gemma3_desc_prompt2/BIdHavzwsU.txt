**Slide Content:**

**Title:** Our Study

**Text content:**

Research gaps:
* Most KD works focus on NLU tasks, or on task-agnostic KD (pre-training).
* All KD for NLG works focus on a single generation task (such as NMT).
* All KD for NLG works consider large datasets with hundreds of thousands of labeled examples and ignore unlabeled data.
    * This is not a realistic or industry-driven setup.

This study:
We conduct a systematic study of task-specific KD for NLG, considering a variety of NLG tasks in realistic setups.

**Slide Content:**

**Knowledge Distillation Recipe**

1. Use an Encoder-decoder model: They are better suited for small-to-medium size fine-tuned models in conditional generation tasks.

2. Prune the decoder layers: This speed up the autoregressive generation process and minimally impact the task performance.

3. If you lack labeled data: Generate with a huge LM (e.g., GPT-4) PTs and use them to fine-tune a medium teacher.

4. Generate with the medium-size teacher multiple PTs via sampling: For the labeled and the unlabeled examples.

5. Employ Logits KD: Augment the training data with PTs and apply Logits KD.

6. Embrace Joint-Teaching: Apply Logits KD not only to PTs generate teacher, but also to PTs generated by the student.
