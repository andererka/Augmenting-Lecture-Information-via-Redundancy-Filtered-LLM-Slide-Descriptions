**Slide Content:**

The slide highlights further advantages of the DeFacto dataset beyond providing a test bed for the proposed NLG tasks. These advantages include enabling better human evaluation due to the inclusion of demonstrations and feedback, facilitating a deeper understanding of factual errors through fine-grained annotations (human-written explanations and instructions), and supporting the training of improved factuality metrics. The dataset's format is also beneficial for more in-depth evaluation of factuality metrics. The dataset has been released on GitHub.
