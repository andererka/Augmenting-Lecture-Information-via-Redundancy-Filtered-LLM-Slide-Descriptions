**Slide Content:**

**Title:** NLPositionality
A framework for characterizing design biases in NLP datasets and models

**Bullet Points:**

*   Datasets and models aggregate judgments and opinions of real people.
*   Positionality can influence research outcomes.
*   NLPositionality framework:
    *   Re-annotate datasets with diverse annotators.
    *   Compare annotations with models and datasets using Pearson's R correlation.
*   Lab in the Wild online experimentation platform.
*   Over 16,000 annotations from over 1000 annotators from 87 countries.
*   Datasets and models are most aligned to English-speaking countries and people with a college education.
*   Some populations are inevitably left behind (e.g., non-binary people).
*   Recommendations:
    *   Record design choices.
    *   Adopt a perspectivist lens.
    *   Build specialized datasets and models.
*   Links to dashboard and paper.
