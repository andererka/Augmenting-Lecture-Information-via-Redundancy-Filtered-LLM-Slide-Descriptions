Task Definition:
Given a set of language instructions describing a floor plan’s intrinsic components, our aim is to generate reasonable 2D floor plan designs that comply with the provided instructions.

Language Instructions:
The balcony located on the Eastern side of the unit, North of the bathrooms, protrudes out from the rest of the unit. It is 4’ wide from West to East, and the outdoor balcony is located Eastward of the Eastmost wall of the bedrooms, common area, and bathrooms. The balcony spans 11’ from North to South, and its Northern wall is shared with the Southern wall of the kitchen. The kitchen and balcony both put out approximately 4’ to the East of the unit.

The Bathroom is located on the central-eastern side of the unit between the common area and the master bedrooms. While the Northern wall of the master bedrooms and the Southern wall of the common area are both roughly 12’ wide.
