**Title:** Our Study

**Text content:**

Research gaps:
* Most KD works focus on NLU tasks, or on task-agnostic KD (pre-training).

This study:

**Slide Content:**

**Knowledge Distillation Recipe**

1. Use an Encoder-decoder model.
2. Prune the decoder layers.
3. If you lack labeled data: Generate pseudo-targets (PTs) with a large language model (LM) and use them to fine-tune a medium teacher.
4. Generate multiple PTs with the medium-size teacher via sampling.
5. Employ Logits KD: Augment the training data with PTs and apply Logits KD.
6. Embrace Joint-Teaching: Apply Logits KD to PTs generated by both the teacher and the student.
