- Main topic: This slide illustrates the architecture of an open-domain question answering (QA) system.
- Key points:
    - The system uses a two-stage process: retrieval and reading.
    - A question is input to a retriever model.
    - The retriever model searches a large knowledge base (Wikipedia) to find relevant documents.
    - The retrieved documents are then passed to a reader model.
    - The reader model extracts the answer from the retrieved documents.
