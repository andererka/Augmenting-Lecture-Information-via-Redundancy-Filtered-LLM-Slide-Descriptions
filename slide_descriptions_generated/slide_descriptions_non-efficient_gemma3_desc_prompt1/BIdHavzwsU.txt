Slide 8
Timestamp: 350.96s
------------------------------
**Slide Content Analysis:**

- **Main topic:** This slide outlines a recipe or set of recommendations for performing knowledge distillation.

- **Key points:**
    * Use an encoder-decoder model: They are better suited for small-to-medium size fine-tuned models in conditional generation tasks.
    * Prune the decoder layers: This speeds up the autoregressive generation process and minimally impacts the task performance.
    * If you lack labeled data: Generate with a huge LM (e.g., GPT-4) and use them to fine-tune a medium teacher.
    * Generate with the medium-size teacher multiple PTs via sampling: For the labeled and the unlabeled examples.
    * Employ Logits KD: Augment the training data with PTs and apply Logits KD.
    * Embrace Joint-Teaching: Apply Logits KD not only to PTs generated teacher, but also to PTs generated by the student.

- **Visual elements:**
    * Numbered list of recommendations.
    * A person is visible in the lower right corner of the slide.
