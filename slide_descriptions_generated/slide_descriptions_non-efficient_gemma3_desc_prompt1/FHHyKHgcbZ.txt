**Slide Content Analysis:**

- **Main topic:** This slide summarizes the key contributions and findings of a research project focused on fact verification using knowledge graphs.

- **Key points:**
    - The research introduces a new dataset, FactKG, designed to facilitate better utilization of knowledge graphs.
    - The dataset contains 108k natural language claims and supports five reasoning types: one-hop, conjunction, existence, multi-hop, and negation.
    - The dataset includes various linguistic patterns, encompassing colloquial and written styles, to enhance practicality.
    - Experimental results demonstrate that incorporating graphical evidence in the model leads to superior performance compared to baseline models.
