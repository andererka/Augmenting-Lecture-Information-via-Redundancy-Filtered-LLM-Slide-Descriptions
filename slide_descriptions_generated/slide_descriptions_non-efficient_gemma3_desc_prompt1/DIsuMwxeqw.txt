**Slide Content Description:**

- **Main topic:** This slide details a method for training a model using noisy pseudo-labels and reducing the noise within them.

- **Key points:**
    *   The goal is to train a model using pseudo-labels while reducing noise in those labels.
    *   Sample re-weighting is used to estimate noise based on the confidence score *s<sup>conf</sup>* and the IoU *s<sup>iou</sup>* of predictions and pseudo-labels, weighting the sample loss accordingly. The formula for the weighted loss *w* is provided: *w = α + (1 - α) * (1 / (1 - s<sup>conf</sup>)) + (1 - α) * (1 / (1 - s<sup>iou</sup>))*
    *   Label refinement is performed: if the model's prediction confidence is high, the prediction is considered a new pseudo-label.

- **Visual elements:**
    *   A flowchart illustrates the training process with noisy pseudo-labels.
    *   The flowchart begins with a "Pseudo Label" box containing two example sentences with bounding box coordinates: "A woman takes shoes off [3.5%, 12.3%]" and "A woman sitting on a bed [16.5%, 17.2%]".
    *   The flow continues to a "Fully Supervised Model" box.
    *   Two branches emerge from the model: "Noise estimation" with a value of 0.9 and "Label refinement" with a value of 0.7.
    *   The "Noise estimation" branch leads to "Reweight loss" with a value of *L<sub>rew</sub>*.
    *   The "Label refinement" branch leads to a refined pseudo-label: "A woman sitting on a bed [4.1%, 18.1%]" and *L<sub>rep</sub>*.

- **Notable text:**
    *   Title: "3. Training with Noisy Pseudo Labels"
    *   Aims: "Train model using pseudo-labels and reduce noise in the pseudo-labels"
    *   Terms: *s<sup>conf</sup>*, *s<sup>iou</sup>*, *w*, *L<sub>rew</sub>*, *L<sub>rep</sub>*
