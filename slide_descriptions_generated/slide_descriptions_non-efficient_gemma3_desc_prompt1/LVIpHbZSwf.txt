**Slide Content Analysis:**

- **Main topic:** This slide explains the MultiAttn method for multimodal fusion, specifically how it learns cross-modal relationships between textual and audio modalities.

- **Key points:**
    - MultiAttn initially learns cross-modal correlations and mapping relationships between textual and audio modalities by treating the textual modality as Query and the audio modality as Key and Value for a bidirectional multi-head cross-attention operation.
    - The output from the first stage is utilized as the new Query, while the visual modality is regarded as Key and Value for another bidirectional multi-head cross-attention layer to fuse the textual modality with visual cues.
    - A feed-forward network consisting of two fully connected layers with a ReLU is adopted, which operates as a key-value memory.

- **Visual elements:**
    - A diagram illustrating a one-layer MultiAttn architecture is present.
    - The diagram shows a sequential flow of operations:
        - Input (labeled as 'x') goes through an Add & Norm layer.
        - The output of Add & Norm goes through a Feed Forward Network (FFN).
        - The output of the FFN goes through a Bidirectional Multi-Head Cross Attention layer (with Query, Key, and Value inputs labeled as Q1, K1, V1).
        - The output of the Cross Attention layer goes through another Add & Norm layer.
        - The output of Add & Norm goes through another Bidirectional Multi-Head Cross Attention layer (with Query, Key, and Value inputs labeled as Q2, K2, V2).

- **Notable text:**
