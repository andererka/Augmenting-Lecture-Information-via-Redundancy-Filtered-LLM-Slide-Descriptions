### **Slide 8 (Timestamp: 350.96s)**
**Title:** *Knowledge Distillation Recipe*
**Text Content:**
- **6 steps:**
  1. Use encoder-decoder models for conditional generation.
  2. Prune decoder layers to speed up autoregressive generation.
  3. If no labeled data: Generate pseudo-targets (PTs) with a large LM (e.g., GPT-4) and fine-tune a medium teacher.
  4. Generate multiple PTs via sampling (labeled + unlabeled).
  5. Apply logits KD, augmenting training data with PTs.
  6. Use joint-teaching: Apply logits KD to PTs from both teacher and student.
