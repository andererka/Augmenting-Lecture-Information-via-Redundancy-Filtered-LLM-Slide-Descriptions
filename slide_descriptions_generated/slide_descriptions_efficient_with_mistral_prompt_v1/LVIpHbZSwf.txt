**Task Definition**

Given a dialogue consisting of *n* utterances \( u_1, ..., u_n \) uttered by speakers \( s_{u_1}, ..., s_{u_n} \), the goal of **Emotion Recognition in Conversations (ERC)** is to predict the emotion label of each utterance from a predefined *k*-class emotion set \( y \). Each utterance \( u_i \) consists of three modalities:
\[ u_i = \{u_i^t, u_i^a, u_i^v\}, \quad i \in \{1, ..., n\} \]

**Slide Content:**
**Problems with Existing Visual Feature Extraction Approaches**
Existing ERC visual feature extractors encode both **facial expressions of interlocutors** and **scene-related information** of each utterance clip.

**Visual Surrounding Information is Redundant:**
* No explicit correlations between scene information and speaker emotion.
* Scene typically remains unchanged throughout the conversation.

**Title:** Visual Feature Extractor - VisExtNet

VisExtNet architecture:
- Input: Two frames (Frame 1, Frame 20).
- Processed by **MTCNN** to extract facial regions.
- Features passed to **VGGFace2 pre-trained ResNet-101**.
- Outputs concatenated and averaged via **Average Pooling** to produce final feature vector \( h_1 \).

MultiAttn consists of three components: **MultiAttn<sub>text</sub>**, **MultiAttn<sub>audio</sub>**, and **MultiAttn<sub>visual</sub>**, each integrating one modality with complementary information from the other two via stacked **bidirectional multi-head cross-attention layers**.

**Slide 10**
Timestamp: 267.47s
**Slide Content:**
**Title:** Multimodal Fusion - MultiAttn

**Text Content:**
- **MultiAttn<sub>text</sub>** learns cross-modal correlations between **text (Query)** and **audio (Key/Value)** via bidirectional multi-head cross-attention.
- Output used as new **Query** for fusion with **visual (Key/Value)**.
- A **feed-forward network (FFN)** with two ReLU-activated layers acts as a key-value memory.

[Figure labeled "Figure 6: Illustration of a one-layer MultiAttn" depicting a tree-like structure with FFN, Add & Norm, and cross-attention layers for text and audio modalities.]

**Limitations**
* VisExtNet does not distinguish between **speakers and irrelevant people** in the scene.
* **SWFC loss** requires large batch sizes on MELD due to class imbalance (computationally expensive).
* Performance in **minority emotions** remains worse than majority classes.
