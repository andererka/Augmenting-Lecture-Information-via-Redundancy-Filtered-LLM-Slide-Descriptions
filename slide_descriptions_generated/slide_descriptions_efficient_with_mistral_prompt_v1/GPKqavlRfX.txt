### **Slide 7 (Analysis: Omission-Based Refinement)**
- **Title:** *Analysis*
- **Content:**
  - **Title:** *Omission-based Summary Refinement*
  - **Graphs (Line Plots):**
    - **Top (BART-large):**
      - **Y-axis:** ROUGE-1 score (30–60).
      - **X-axis:** Datasets (SAM, Dial, Email, QM, Tweet).
      - **Lines:**
        - **Raw (Yellow):** Baseline summaries.
        - **+Dial (Orange):** Summaries + dialogue context.
        - **+Omit (Red):** Summaries + detected omissions.
      - **Key Observation:** **+Omit** improves ROUGE-1 scores across all datasets (e.g., Dial: 46.5 → 51.5).
    - **Bottom (T5-small):**
      - Same structure as top graph.
      - **Key Observation:** **+Omit** improves ROUGE-1 scores (e.g., Dial: 45.6 → 51.1).
  - **Key Takeaway:** Omission detection enhances summary quality.
