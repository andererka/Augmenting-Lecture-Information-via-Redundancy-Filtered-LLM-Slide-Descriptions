### Slide 8 (Methods)
**Title:** Methods
- **Four colored circles (2x2 grid):**
  1. **Blue:** Context-retrieval for short-context models (retrieves relevant transcript segments).
  2. **Green:** Multi-span models (token classification: `/` = in span, `O` = outside).
  3. **Orange:** Single-span models (outputs a single "super" span from first to last relevant sentence).
  4. **Purple:** Silver data augmentation (auto-annotated answers from MediaSum interviews).
- **Citation:** Zhu et al. (2021) at the bottom.
